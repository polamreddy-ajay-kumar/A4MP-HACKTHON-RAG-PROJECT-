SAMPLE STUDENT HANDBOOK

1. Attendance
Students must have at least 75% attendance in every course to be eligible for the semester examinations. A student whose attendance is between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation by paying the prescribed fee and submitting a valid reason, such as a medical certificate. Students with attendance below 65% are detained and must repeat the semester. Attendance is recorded for every theory class and every laboratory session, and the monthly attendance report is displayed on the department notice board.

2. Examinations
Each course has an internal assessment of 30 marks and a semester end examination of 70 marks. Two mid examinations are conducted in every semester and the better of the two is considered for the internal marks. A student must score at least 40% in the semester end examination and 50% overall to pass a course. Students who fail may appear for the supplementary examinations that are conducted within three months of the declaration of results.

3. Library
The central library is open from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM on all working days. Every student can borrow up to three books at a time for a period of 14 days. A fine of Rs. 2 per day is charged for each book returned after the due date. Lost books must be replaced with the same edition or the current price of the book must be paid. Reference books and journals can be read only inside the library.

4. Internships and Projects
Every MCA student must complete a short-term internship of at least four weeks after the second semester. The internship must be done in a recognised industry or training organisation, and the student must submit an internship report prepared under the guidance of a faculty member along with the completion certificate. The final semester project work is evaluated through a review by the department committee and a viva voce examination.

5. Fee Payment
Tuition fee must be paid in two instalments, one at the beginning of each semester. A late fee of Rs. 100 per week is charged if the fee is paid after the last date. Scholarship holders must submit their scholarship sanction order to the accounts section within one month of joining. Fee receipts must be preserved and shown whenever the college office asks for them.

6. Code of Conduct
Students must wear their identity cards inside the campus at all times. Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and is punishable under the law. Mobile phones must be kept silent during class hours and laboratory sessions. Damage to college property must be compensated by the student responsible. Students are expected to be punctual, polite and respectful towards faculty members, staff and fellow students.

7. Grievance Redressal
Any student having a complaint about academic or administrative matters can submit it in writing to the grievance redressal committee. The committee reviews each complaint within seven working days and informs the student about the action taken. Complaints related to harassment are handled by the internal complaints committee, and the identity of the complainant is kept confidential.
